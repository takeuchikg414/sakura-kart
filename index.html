CAUTION

A valid driving permit for Japan is required to join the tour. Please check the “Requirements” tab before booking.

Our guide leads your group through iconic Tokyo spots — Tokyo Tower, Rainbow Bridge, Shibuya and more. Choose your course, pick a costume, and enjoy the city from the driver’s seat.

SAKURA KART Tokyo shop

Bring one of the following sets on the day of your tour:

  1. International Driving Permit (IDP) 1949 Geneva Convention only + your home license + passport
  2. Home license + official Japanese translation For eligible countries (e.g. Switzerland, Germany, France, Taiwan) + passport
  3. Japanese driving license Standard or medium vehicle license
  4. US SOFA license SOFA license + US state license + military ID
  1. Arrive at the shop 30 minutes before your tour time.
  2. Show your booking name, driving permit and passport at reception.
  3. Sign the waiver and pick your favorite costume.
  4. Store your belongings in a locker.
  5. Safety briefing and practice drive with your guide.
  6. Let’s go! Enjoy the tour.
What if it rains?
Tours run in light rain — raincoats are provided. In severe weather we will contact you to reschedule or refund.
Can passengers ride with me?
Karts are single-seat. Every participant needs a valid driving permit.

FAQ Frequently asked questions

What do I need to drive?

A valid driving permit for Japan, such as an International Driving Permit (1949 Geneva Convention). See the Requirements tab in the booking section.

Is there an age limit?

Drivers must hold a valid permit for Japan, which in practice means 18 years or older.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Free changes up to 3 days before your tour. Please see our cancellation policy for details.

What should I wear?

Closed-toe shoes are required. Costumes are free to rent at the shop.
